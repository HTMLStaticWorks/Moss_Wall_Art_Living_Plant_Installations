Frequently Asked

Is installation included?

For "Statement" and "Living Wall" packages, professional on-site installation is included within a 100-mile radius of our studios. Shipping is available worldwide.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, for projects over $5,000, we offer a 50/50 payment structure or 6-month interest-free financing for residential clients.

Can I customize the frame materials?

Absolutely. While Walnut and Oak are our standards, we can source exotic woods, brushed metals, or powder-coated steel to match your interior hardware.
