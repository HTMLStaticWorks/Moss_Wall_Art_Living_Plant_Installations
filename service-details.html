Preserved Moss Walls:
Timeless Artistry.

Our preserved moss walls are the pinnacle of biophilic luxury. Using real, harvested moss that has been stabilized through a proprietary eco-friendly process, we create vertical art that remains soft, vibrant, and tactile for years—all without the need for water, sunlight, or soil.

Each panel is a bespoke composition of varied moss species, including Pole Moss, Flat Moss, and Lichen, meticulously arranged to create depth, movement, and a sense of forest-floor immersion.

The Artisanal Process

01

Harvest & Preserve

Moss is ethically harvested at its peak. We replace its sap with a natural preservation liquid that keeps it eternally fresh.

02

Architectural Framing

Creative Composition

Common Inquiries

Does it really require NO water?

Yes, exactly. The moss is preserved, meaning it is no longer biologically active. It stays soft and green without a single drop of water.

What is the expected lifespan?

Typically 8-10 years. In optimal indoor conditions (40-60% humidity), it can last even longer.

Can I install it in my bathroom?

We recommend against high-humidity areas like showers, as excessive moisture can damage the preservation. However, a well-ventilated bathroom is fine.
