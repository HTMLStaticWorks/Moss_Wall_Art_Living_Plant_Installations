Our Journey

Rooted in Nature, Designed for Life.

Founded in 2018 in a small botanical studio in Copenhagen, Verdant Walls was born from a simple observation: modern interiors were becoming increasingly disconnected from the natural world.

What started as a passion for terrarium design quickly evolved into a full-scale botanical installation firm. Today, we bridge the gap between architectural precision and organic chaos.

Sustainability First
